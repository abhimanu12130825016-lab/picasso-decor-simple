FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about panel installation, maintenance, or visiting our Kolkata location?

What materials are Picasso Decor panels made of?

Our fluted panels and UV sheets are manufactured using high-density WPC (Wood Plastic Composite), PVC, and SPC compounds. They combine polymer durability with realistic wood grain, marble, or metallic finishes.

Are these panels suitable for Kolkata's damp monsoon weather?

Yes. Unlike traditional plywood or MDF which can absorb moisture and warp, our WPC and UV sheet collections are 100% waterproof, termite-proof, and fire-retardant, making them ideal for West Bengal conditions.

Can panels be installed on existing walls without chipping plaster?

Absolutely. Panels can be mounted directly onto existing painted walls, cement plaster, or wooden framing using silicone adhesive, construction adhesive, or hidden screws without dusty masonry work.

How can I view samples and get exact pricing for my project?

You can visit our Kolkata showroom to view full-size display boards and physical sample sets, or contact us directly via Google Maps for location guidance and catalog details.
